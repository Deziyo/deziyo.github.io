凛冬将至

08 · 短篇速览
凛冬将至 披上外套，拉紧兜帽，七零八落的声音像尖锐的字条在我眼前漫天飞舞，我尽量避开这场雨...... 可天总是不晴，它愈下愈大，越来越暗，小巷子里的路灯都比它亮。它将我的外套湿透，我把手插进口袋——这里真的好冷。 我再次抬起头，去接受那些声音，不得已驻足观望。看着看着，没有哪怕一条与我相干，可我已经无法再向前了。 在那一刻，再怎么坚定的信念也会动摇的。为什么我要淋这场雨，它从哪里来，谁放的，又会飘到哪去，我很困惑。 “如果你有了篝火，你至少能烘干你的衣服，暖暖你的身子”——贝尔·格里尔斯 Do you still not get it? One year from now, three years, ten years—what will this place have left you with? Tell me… nothing. Absolutely nothing. Turn around and look behind you—your family, your friends, the people who once pulled you out of the dark. When you step through the front door, your parents are there, all smiles like a warm spring breeze. When you’re drifting with nothing to do, there are friends ready to drag you across the world just to keep you moving. When you’re lost, there’s always that one person who shows up with a sliver of hope… So tell me—what exactly are you doing, Deziyo? And then it hits me—I might just be a ridiculous man. A stupid one, even. Or maybe I’m not even that yet… just a kid. A tiny, barely-170cm nobody. Haha… yeah, that’s supposed to be a joke. Funny, right? But hearing it come out of my own mouth still hurts. 我想离开，就这样消失，然后拿起相机...在那之前我会做好一切，所以，再见了很多人
